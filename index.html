Common questions

Everything you need
to know

How often should I log? Is it just once a day?
+
Log whenever your activity or energy changes — not just once. Most users log 4–8 times per day: morning, after meetings, lunch, deep work sessions, afternoon, evening. Tap the + button whenever something shifts. The more you log, the more accurate your patterns become.
What does "Log Activity" actually mean?
+
A 4-tap check-in under 10 seconds: 1) What are you doing right now? 2) Energy level 1–5. 3) Productive or not? 4) Mood or context. Elevra builds your pattern profile from these micro-logs over time.
When does Elevra generate my reflection?
+
Automatically — after you log if you have 2+ logs. Manually — tap Refresh on the Insights screen anytime. You get 2 free reflections per day. Extra ones cost 2 credits each. Share any insight to earn +1 free credit instantly.
What do the thumbs up / thumbs down buttons do?
+
Thumbs up shows "Glad it resonated" — Elevra uses your positive reaction to make future reflections more like what you liked. Thumbs down opens a short feedback form — Elevra adjusts its tone and content for future insights. No email is sent. It is a private AI improvement loop.
How do I earn credits without paying?
+
Tap Share Insight at the bottom of any reflection card — Elevra gives you +1 free credit instantly as a thank-you. You also get 2 free reflections every day that reset at midnight, which does not cost credits.
What is the Comm Coach and do I need it?
+
The Comm Coach is an optional AI speech analyser — record 10–90 seconds of speech and get scored across 5 pillars. Completely separate from daily logging and free to use. Best for presentations, meeting prep, or daily communication practice.

Activity logs, AI-generated insights, credits usage, and basic account info (email or Apple ID). Audio from Comm Coach is processed in real-time and not stored permanently.

Your logs are sent to Claude (Anthropic) to generate personalized insights. We do not use your data to train AI models. Data sent is anonymised to the extent possible.

All data is stored securely on Supabase servers with industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. Your logs are private and never shared with third parties.
